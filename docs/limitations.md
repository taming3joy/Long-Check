# Limitations

Long-Check is a checklist assistant, not a guaranteed scam detector.

- It does not detect scams with 100% accuracy.
- It does not inspect blockchain transactions.
- It does not connect to wallets.
- It does not verify official domains automatically yet.
- It does not scrape websites or social channels.
- It cannot confirm whether a project, token, or sender is legitimate from text alone.

The goal is to help users pause, notice common risk signals, and verify through official sources before risky actions.
